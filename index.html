What services do you offer?+

We offer oil changes, mechanical services, tire service and storage, diagnostics, brakes, A/C service, detailing, windshield replacement, paint protection film, and window tinting. Tell us what you need when requesting an appointment.

How do I book an appointment?+

Call 587-220-8287 or use the appointment request form below. We will need your vehicle details and the service you have in mind. A request is not a confirmed booking; please wait for the team to confirm a time.

Which vehicles can you work on?+

Send us your vehicle’s make, model, year, and the work you need. We will confirm whether the service is available for your particular vehicle before arranging a visit.

When do you open, and what are your hours?+

Our grand opening is October 12. Regular business hours will be posted here soon. Please call to confirm availability before visiting.

Do you provide a warranty?+

Please ask about the warranty terms that apply to your specific parts and service before authorizing work. Warranty details will be added here once finalized.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Payment options will be confirmed before opening. Call or email us to check which methods are available before your appointment.

Where is Everest Automotive located?+

Find us at 3114 16 Avenue North, Lethbridge, Alberta. Use the Get Directions button below to plan your route.

Can I get an estimate before work begins?+

Yes—contact us with your vehicle details and your concerns to request an estimate. Some issues need an inspection or diagnosis first; ask the team what is involved before proceeding.
